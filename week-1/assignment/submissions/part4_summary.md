## Effect of increasing E on frequency

```python
k = np.sqrt(2*(E-V))
```
As E increases (given its already E>V) we see k increases

```python
phi(x) = np.cos(k*x)
```
As k increases the wavelength gets shorter and frequency increases

## What does a rapidly decaying function mean?

`phi(x) = e^-k*x`

A larger decay means phi would become zero very quickly.
As probability is proportional to phi(x)^2 
The probability of finding an electron becomes very small
